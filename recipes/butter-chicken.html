Butter Chicken

Prep time: 15mins | Cook time: 45mins | Total Time: 1hr
Total Servings: 4

About

Butter Chicken, a dish everyone knows from the ❤️, originates from India in the late 1950s.
This fusion of simple ingredients into a gravy alongside a tandoor-cooked marinated chicken, pops flavors not comprehensible.
To surprise your friends and family, learn the recipe in a jiffy just by following a few steps.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, divided to two halves
  • 1 onion, minced(preferred) or diced
  • 1 tablespoon garlic, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce(homemade preferred)
  • 3 cup heavy cream
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon hot chilli pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 700 grams, boneless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized chunks
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons tandoori masala
  • 1 cup curd

Steps

  1. Cover the chicken with mix of curd, and tandoori masala and set aside for atleast half an hour, to enrichen the flavor(No need to wait if time is limited).
  2. Gather the remaining ingredients and pre-heat a skillet or a pan(Whatever is available for you).
  3. In the cookware, melt the butter slabs(leave some for later use) and toss in the onions and garlic. Stir the ingredients until the onion caramelizes to dark brown, for about 15 minutes.
  4. Combine the remaining butter, cream, tomato sauce, salt, hot chilli powder in a sauce-pan, and cook till simmer.
  5. Reduce heat on sauce-pan to medium or low, cover, and simmer, stirring occassionally in between, for next 30 minutes. Mix in the caramelized onions.
  6. Pre-heat oil in a dutch-oven or wok or something similar, and toss in the marinated chicken pieces.
  7. Keep frying the chicken until no pinkish color is left over the pieces, for about 10 minutes.
  8. Add the cooked chicken to the sauce and simmer it for 5 minutes before serving(Can add a whip of cream or parsley as garnishing).
