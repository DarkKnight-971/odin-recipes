About

Butter Chicken, a dish everyone knows from the ❤️, originates from India in the late 1950s.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, divided to two halves
  • 1 onion, minced(preferred) or diced
  • 1 tablespoon garlic, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce(homemade preferred)
  • 3 cup heavy cream
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon hot chilli pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 700 grams, boneless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized chunks
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons tandoori masala
  • 1 cup curd
