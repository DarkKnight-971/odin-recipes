About

Tandoori chicken dates its origins from the lost civilization of Harappa around 3000 BC.
With the modern fling of roasting them over charcoal-heated ovens or tandoors, and a sprinkle of lemon juice, this culinary masterpiece
transcends time and of course, the palate of all food-savvies alike.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chopped chicken(medium or large size preferred)
  • 1 lemon-juice
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons garam masala
  • 2 cups yogurt or curd(as per your dietary needs allows)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, finely grated
  • 1 teaspoon hot chilli pepper

  • Optional

    • 1 teaspoon red food color
    • 1 teaspoon yellow food color

  • Garnishing

    • chopped cilantro or parsley
    • lemon slices, cut in wedges

Steps

  1. Wash and rinse the chopped chicken thoroughly in cold water. Make slits into the meat and place inside a shallow cookware or similar dish.
  2. Season the chicken with csalt and lemon. Set it aside for 20 to 30 minutes.
  3. Mix onion, garlic, ginger, garam masala, curd and hot chilli pepper in a medium-sized bowl until the mixture is smooth.(Then stir in the food colors
  4. Spread the mixture over chicken, cover with a lid, and let it refrigerate for 6 hours or more.(The longer, the better)
  5. When ready to cook, pre-heat the outdoor-grill and oil the grates.
  6. Place the chicken over the grates and discard excess marinate mixture.
  7. Cook the chicken till no pinkinesh remains in the chicken and all juice runs dry.(An instant read-thermometer near the bone should read 165 degrees F or 74 degrees C)
  8. Garnish with cilantro and lemon wedges.
