Chana Masala

Prep time: 15mins | Cook time: 15mins | Total Time: 30mins
Total Servings: 2

About

Chickpea, or chana as called in Indian subcontinent, has been in trade over the centuries around the world.
Being a rich source of carbs and proteins for vegetarians, chickpeas with simple masalas to fray creates a variety of
culinary marvel, like chana masala, chhole bhature or even salad, to name a few.

Ingredients

  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 chopped tomato
  • 1 small-chunk of ginger(about an inch), skinned and chopped
  • 4 to 6 cloves of garlic finely chopped
  • 1 green chilli pepper. chopped alongside seeds within
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 fresh bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon hot chilli pepper
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 pinch salt, or enough to taste
  • water, as needed
  • ½ kg chickpea, drained(if raw, bathe in water for 8 hrs before use)

  • Garnishing

    • fresh cilantro or parsley leaves

Steps

  1. Grind onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, and green chilli pepper in a food processor into a mushy paste(use water to loosen up the ingredients).
  2. Pre-heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or a dutch-oven or somthing similar. Fry the bay leaves in oil for about 30 seconds, to enrichen the flavor.
  3. Drop the paste into the oil and cook until oil separates from the mixture, and is golden color, for about 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Season the mixture with hot chilli pepper, garam masala, turmeric, and salt; cook and stir for about 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. Pour enough water to make the mixture a thick sauce; bring to a boil and add chickpeas into the sauce.
  6. Reduce heat to medium and cook until the chicpeas are heated thoroughly, for about 5 to 7 minutes.
  7. Garnish with cilantro.
